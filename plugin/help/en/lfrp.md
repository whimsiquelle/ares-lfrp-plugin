# Looking for RP

The `lfrp` command marks your character as currently looking for RP. Other players can see that you are available and may contact you for a scene.

You may list yourself for any type of scene, or specify that you are looking for TXT-only, live-only, or async-only RP.

Your LFRP status expires automatically after a set number of hours. If you do not specify a time, the game uses the default duration.

## Commands

`lfrp`

Marks you as looking for any type of scene using the default duration.

`lfrp <hours>`

Marks you as looking for any type of scene for the specified number of hours.

Example:

`lfrp 4`

Marks you as looking for any type of scene for 4 hours.

`lfrp/txt`

Marks you as looking for TXT scenes only using the default duration.

`lfrp/txt <hours>`

Marks you as looking for TXT scenes only for the specified number of hours.

Example:

`lfrp/txt 2`

Marks you as looking for TXT scenes only for 2 hours.

`lfrp/live`

Marks you as looking for live scenes only using the default duration.

`lfrp/live <hours>`

Marks you as looking for live scenes only for the specified number of hours.

Example:

`lfrp/live 3`

Marks you as looking for live scenes only for 3 hours.

`lfrp/async`

Marks you as looking for async scenes only using the default duration.

`lfrp/async <hours>`

Marks you as looking for async scenes only for the specified number of hours.

Example:

`lfrp/async 8`

Marks you as looking for async scenes only for 8 hours.

`lfrp/stop`

Removes your looking-for-RP status.

`lfrp/list`

Shows characters currently marked as looking for RP, the type of scene they requested, their current online status, and when their LFRP status expires.

## Scene Types

**Any Scene** means you are open to TXT, live, or async RP.

**TXT Only** means you are specifically looking for a TXT scene.

**Live Only** means you are looking for a scene where players are present and posting in real time or close to real time.

**Async Only** means you are looking for a slower scene where players may respond over a longer period of time.

Choose the option that best matches what you are available to write right now. Selecting a new LFRP option replaces your previous LFRP status.

## Web Portal

You may also set or remove your LFRP status through the Looking for RP panel on the website sidebar.

The web portal offers the same options:

* Any Scene
* TXT Only
* Live Only
* Async Only

The website list updates automatically while you are viewing the portal.

## Notes

The looking-for-RP status is temporary.

The game may limit how many hours you can set at once.

Your character remains visible on the LFRP list until the status expires or you remove it manually, even if you log off or leave the web portal.

Being listed as looking for RP is an invitation for someone to contact you about a possible scene. It does not require you to accept every pitch or start a scene immediately.
